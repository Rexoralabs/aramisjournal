

# Luxe Journal — AI-Powered Mindful Journaling

A premium, dark-themed PWA for daily journaling with an intelligent AI companion that helps you reflect, tracks your emotional patterns, and celebrates your consistency.

---

## 🎨 Design Direction

**Luxury Dark Aesthetic**
- Deep charcoal/obsidian backgrounds with subtle gradients
- Warm gold/amber accent colors for highlights and interactions
- Elegant serif typography for headings, clean sans-serif for body
- Generous white space and smooth micro-animations
- Glassmorphism cards with soft glows

---

## 📱 Core Features

### 1. Daily Journal Entry
- Beautiful full-screen writing experience with distraction-free mode
- Date-stamped entries with optional mood selection
- Auto-save as you type
- Rich text formatting (bold, italic, lists)

### 2. AI Journaling Companion
- **Daily Prompts**: Personalized thought-provoking questions to inspire your writing
- **Mood Analysis**: AI detects emotional tone from your entries and tracks patterns
- **Weekly Reflections**: AI-generated summaries highlighting themes, growth, and insights

### 3. Streak & Motivation System
- **Streak Counter**: Prominent display of current daily streak
- **Calendar View**: Visual monthly grid showing journaling days with streak highlights
- **Milestone Badges**: Unlock achievements at 7, 30, 60, 100, 365 days with celebratory animations

### 4. Journal History
- Scrollable timeline of past entries
- Search and filter by date or mood
- Quick-read cards with entry previews

---

## 🔐 Account & Cloud Sync

- Email/password signup and login
- Secure cloud storage for all entries
- Seamless sync across devices
- PWA installable to home screen

---

## 📄 Pages

1. **Home/Today** — Daily prompt, quick entry, streak display
2. **Write** — Full journaling editor with AI prompts
3. **History** — Browse past entries with calendar view
4. **Insights** — AI mood trends, weekly summaries, streak milestones
5. **Profile** — Account settings, theme preferences
